Our Story

Bread baked with intention, shared with love.

The House Standard started the way most good things do — at a kitchen table, with a jar of starter, and a belief that we can do better. Better than preservatives. Better than mass production. Better than bread that forgets what bread is supposed to be.

Every loaf we bake is a small act of resistance against the rushed and the processed. We believe in slow food, sustainable living, and the quiet joy of sharing something made by hand.

🌾
Real Ingredients Only Flour, water, salt, and time. That's it. No dough conditioners, no shortcuts.
🌱
Sustainably Minded Minimal packaging, locally sourced when possible, zero waste in our process.
🏡
Community First We're neighbors baking for neighbors. This is what abundant living looks like.

How We Bake

The slow way
is the right way.

We don't rush. Every loaf follows a process refined over hundreds of bakes. The result is bread with real flavor, real texture, and real nutrition — the kind your gut actually thanks you for.

🌾
1

Mill & Mix

We start with quality flour and our living starter, mixing by hand to feel the dough come alive.

⏳
2

Long Ferment

72 hours cold fermentation develops complex flavor and breaks down gluten for better digestion.

✋
3

Shape & Score

Each loaf is hand-shaped and scored — the pattern guides the oven spring and makes each loaf unique.

🔥
4

Dutch Oven Bake

Baked in a sealed Dutch oven to trap steam — giving that crackling crust and tender, open crumb.
